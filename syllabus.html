Five generated BFSI datasets. Each was built to teach a specific failure mode rather than to be clean.

DatasetRowsThe trap
credit_risk12,000MNAR missingness, a post-outcome leakage column, macro drift in year three
card_fraud60,0000.7% positives; fraud clusters on compromised cards, so a random split inflates by ~34%
insurance_claims15,00091% zeros; squared error predicts negative claim costs, Tweedie does not
telco_churn8,000the classic blank total_charges on brand-new accounts
support_tickets6,0005% label noise and 16% genuinely ambiguous text — a real accuracy ceiling

If you only do four

  1. 04 — Validation Strategy. A wrong split moves your metric by twenty points and nothing warns you. Everything downstream is measured through it.
  2. 08 — Metrics, Calibration, Thresholds. The one people skip most and benefit from most. The model emits a score; the business needs a decision.
  3. 12 — Interpretability and Fairness. Where a model meets a committee, and where the binding constraint turns out to be legal rather than statistical.
  4. 14 — Capstone. All of it at once, then exercise 14.4 attacks the recommendation the way a validator will.
